# 👥 HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard

An interactive human resources dashboard designed to visualize workforce demographics, identify key drivers of employee turnover, and support retention strategy across 1,400+ personnel.

---

## 📌 Key Workforce Metrics
* **Core HR KPIs:** Evaluated **1,417** total workforce, **1,186** active employees, **231** attritions (**16.30% attrition rate**), **36.94** average age, and **11.3 years** average tenure.
* **Department & Role Breakdown:** Mapped attrition patterns across Sales, Operations, HR, IT, Finance, and Administration against employee satisfaction scores.
* **Demographics & Compensation:** Segmented turnover across salary brackets (0–3 LPA up to 10+ LPA), experience levels, and gender.
* **Business Impact:** Identified high-risk attrition groups to enable targeted retention initiatives and reduce replacement costs.
